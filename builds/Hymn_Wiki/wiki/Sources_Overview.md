# Sources

The Hymn Wiki draws on nine primary sources spanning three centuries of hymnological scholarship. All are in the public domain.

## Primary Sources

| Source | Author | Year | Contribution |
|--------|--------|------|-------------|
| **[[The_Christian_Hymn_Book]]** | Alexander Campbell | 1870 | All 1,324 hymn texts with meter, topic, and scripture references |
| **[[King_James_Bible]]** | --- | 1611 | Scripture verse text integrated at hymn level (458 linkages) |

## Historical & Biographical Studies

| Source | Author | Year | Contribution |
|--------|--------|------|-------------|
| **[[The_Story_of_Our_Hymns]]** | Ernest Edwin Ryden | 1930 | Chronological backbone: 150+ biographical profiles organized by tradition |
| **[[The_Story_of_the_Hymns_and_Tunes]]** | Theron Brown & Hezekiah Butterworth | 1906 | Musical and thematic depth: tune histories, composition stories |
| **[[The_English_Hymn]]** | Louis F. Benson | 1915 | Institutional history: how each denomination adopted hymn-singing |
| **[[English_Hymns_Their_Authors_and_History]]** | Samuel W. Duffield | 1886 | Hymn encyclopedia: 1,081 entries organized by first line |
| **[[Baptist_Hymn_Writers]]** | Henry S. Burrage | 1888 | 200+ Baptist hymn writers worldwide, Anabaptist martyrs through 1888 |
| **[[The_Hymns_and_Hymn_Writers_of_the_Church]]** | Charles S. Nutter & Wilbur F. Tillett | 1911 | Annotated Methodist Hymnal: 383 hymns with scholarly notes, 306 author profiles |
| **[[American_Writers_and_Compilers_of_Sacred_Music]]** | Frank J. Metcalf | 1925 | 70+ American tune composers: biographies, publications, tune-to-hymn pairings |

## How Sources Work Together

The sources complement each other by design:

- **Campbell** provides the hymn texts themselves --- the raw material
- **Ryden** and **Butterworth-Brown** provide the biographical and narrative context
- **Benson** provides the institutional and denominational framework
- **Duffield** provides hymn-by-hymn detail: composition stories, textual variants, anecdotes
- **Burrage** provides the Baptist perspective missing from other sources
- **Nutter** provides scholarly annotation and myth-debunking
- **Metcalf** provides the tune-and-composer data that connects text to music
- **The KJV** provides the scriptural foundation underlying the hymns

When sources disagree on dates, attributions, or facts, both accounts are noted with source attribution on the relevant wiki page.
