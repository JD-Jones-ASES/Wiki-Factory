# Hymn Wiki

A comprehensive guide to Christian hymns --- their stories, their writers, and the Scripture that inspired them.

## What's Inside

This wiki covers **1,324 hymns** from the Christian tradition, spanning from the earliest church chants to the gospel songs of the early twentieth century. For each hymn you'll find the full text, its historical context, and the Bible passages that connect to it.

## Sources

This wiki draws on four primary sources:

1. **[[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]]** (1870) --- Alexander Campbell's collection of 1,324 hymns
2. **[[The_Story_of_Our_Hymns|The Story of Our Hymns]]** (1930) --- Ernest Edwin Ryden's biographical and chronological history
3. **[[The_Story_of_the_Hymns_and_Tunes|The Story of the Hymns and Tunes]]** (1906) --- Theron Brown and Hezekiah Butterworth's narrative history
4. **[[King_James_Bible|The King James Bible]]** --- scripture references for every hymn

All source texts are in the public domain. Hymn texts are reproduced from Campbell's 1870 collection.
