# John Hart Stockton

John Hart Stockton (1813–1877) was a minister in the Methodist Episcopal Church, a hymn writer, and a composer of tunes.

## Life and Career

Stockton was born in 1813 and died in 1877. He was a member of the New Jersey Annual Conference of the Methodist Episcopal Church, and filled various pastoral charges as a member of that Conference. He was not only a preacher but a musician and composer of tunes, as well as a hymn writer. He published two gospel song books: Salvation Melodies (1874) and Precious Songs (1875).

## Hymns and Tunes in the Methodist Hymnal

Stockton contributed as both author and composer:

- Hymn: "Come, every soul by sin oppressed"
- Tune: Invodale (a melody he composed)

## Methodist Hymnal Context (Nutter)

Nutter and Tillett place Stockton in the tradition of Methodist minister-musicians who worked both sides of the hymnal — writing texts and composing tunes. His gospel song books fit the pattern of the late nineteenth-century Methodist holiness movement, which produced a large body of accessible popular sacred music for use in revival and camp meeting settings. His colleague [[William_McDonald]] worked in a similar mode.
