# Frederick Lucian Hosmer

Frederick Lucian Hosmer (1840–1929) was an American Unitarian minister and hymn writer, one of the notable contributors of new hymns to the late nineteenth-century American tradition.

## Life and Career

Hosmer was born in Framingham, Massachusetts, in 1840. He graduated from Harvard College in 1862 and from the Divinity School, Cambridge, in 1869. He served as pastor of Unitarian Churches at Quincy, Illinois (1872–77); Cleveland, Ohio (1878–92); St. Louis (1894–99); and later at Berkeley, California, where he resided. He was one of the editors of the Unity Hymns in 1880.

In 1908 Dr. Hosmer delivered a course of lectures at Harvard University on "Church Hymnody." Among his published volumes were The Way of Life (1877) and The Thought of God in Hymns and Poems (first series, 1885; second series, 1894).

## Hymns in the Methodist Hymnal

Three of Hosmer's hymns appear in the Methodist Hymnal:

- "I little see, I little know"
- "Not always on the mount may we"
- "O thou in all thy might so far"

## Methodist Hymnal Context (Nutter)

Nutter and Tillett recognize Hosmer as a Unitarian whose hymns found their way into Methodist worship — a testimony to the broad appeal of his devotional verse. His hymns are reflective and quietly searching in character, suited to moments of honest spiritual self-examination.
