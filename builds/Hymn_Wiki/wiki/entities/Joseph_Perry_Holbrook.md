# Joseph Perry Holbrook

Joseph Perry Holbrook (1822–1888) was an American composer of hymn tunes and music editor who played an important role in preparing the Methodist Hymnal of 1878.

## Life and Career

Holbrook was born near Boston, Massachusetts. He was the editor of Songs of the Sanctuary and was associated with Dr. Eben Tourjee in editing the Methodist Hymnal of 1878. He served as compiler and editor of several books of music.

## Tunes in the Methodist Hymnal

Holbrook contributed two tunes to the Methodist Hymnal:

- Truman
- Greek Hymn

## Methodist Hymnal Context (Nutter)

Nutter and Tillett identify Holbrook as one of the musicians who shaped the Methodist Hymnal at an important transitional moment — the 1878 revision that preceded the major 1905 edition. His collaboration with Eben Tourjee, the founder of the New England Conservatory, placed him in the same professional circle as [[Lowell_Mason]]'s successors in American church music reform.
