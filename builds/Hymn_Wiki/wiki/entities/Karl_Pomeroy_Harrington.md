# Karl Pomeroy Harrington

Karl Pomeroy Harrington (1861–1953) was an American educator, musician, and hymn tune composer who served as one of the music editors of the Methodist Hymnal of 1905.

## Life and Career

Harrington was born in Somersworth, New Hampshire, the son of Calvin Sears Harrington. He was a Professor of Latin at Wesleyan University from 1905 onward. His work spanned both classical scholarship and sacred music — an unusual combination that placed him in the tradition of the scholar-musician.

## Tunes in the Methodist Hymnal

According to Nutter and Tillett, Harrington contributed thirteen tunes to the Methodist Hymnal as one of its music editors:

Worship, Praise, Parker, Christmas Song, Weihnacht, Copeland, Middletown, Orono, Beyond, Evanston, Palm Sunday, More, and Invocation Sentence.

## Methodist Hymnal Context (Nutter)

Harrington and his co-editor [[Peter_Christian_Lutkin]] were both academic musicians at major Methodist-connected universities — Wesleyan and Northwestern respectively. Their appointment as music editors of the 1905 Hymnal represented a deliberate effort to bring professional musical standards and fresh composition to the collection. Harrington's thirteen tunes spread across a wide range of worship occasions, from praise to seasonal (Palm Sunday, Christmas) to liturgical (Invocation Sentence).
