# William McDonald

William McDonald (1820–1901) was a minister of the Methodist Episcopal Church, a prominent member of the National Holiness Association, and a hymn writer and composer.

## Life and Career

McDonald was born at Belmont, Maine, in 1820. He joined the Miami Conference in 1843 and served various pastoral charges in the North and West. He was a prominent member of the National Holiness Association. For several years he was the editor of the Christian Witness, published in Boston. From 1870 until his death he did much evangelistic work. He was interested in Church music and published several small volumes of hymns for social worship. He died in 1901.

## Hymns and Tunes in the Methodist Hymnal

McDonald contributed both as a hymn writer and as a composer. His hymn "I am coming to the cross" appears in the collection. As a composer, his tune "Going Home" was also included.

## Methodist Hymnal Context (Nutter)

Nutter and Tillett present McDonald as a figure who straddled the roles of minister, holiness movement leader, editor, and music maker — a combination typical of the Methodist holiness wing in the post–Civil War period. His association with the National Holiness Association and the Christian Witness placed him at the heart of the late nineteenth-century Wesleyan holiness revival.
