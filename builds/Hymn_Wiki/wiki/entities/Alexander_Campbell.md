# Alexander Campbell

**1788-1866** --- Irish-born American minister, educator, and one of the founders of the Restoration Movement (Disciples of Christ / Churches of Christ).

## Life

Born in County Antrim, Ireland, Campbell emigrated to the United States in 1809. He became a leading voice for Christian unity and a return to New Testament patterns of worship. He founded Bethany College in what is now West Virginia and was a prolific writer and debater.

## The Christian Hymn Book

Campbell's most lasting contribution to hymnody was his compilation of [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book]], first published in the 1840s and revised in 1865 by a committee. The collection contains **1,324 hymns** organized by theological theme, drawing from the best of English Protestant hymnody --- especially the works of [[Isaac Watts]], [[Charles Wesley]], [[Philip Doddridge]], and [[William Cowper]].

The hymnal served the Restoration Movement for decades and is the primary source for all hymn texts in this wiki.

## Significance

While not a hymn writer himself, Campbell's editorial judgment shaped the worship music of an entire denomination. His decision to organize hymns by doctrinal subject rather than liturgical season reflected the Restoration Movement's emphasis on Scripture and reason.
