# Thomas Jones of Denbigh

Thomas Jones of Denbigh represents the generation of Welsh Calvinistic Methodist ministers who consolidated the revival tradition of the eighteenth century and carried it into the nineteenth. His hymns share the theological seriousness and pastoral warmth of the movement at its best.

## Life

Jones was born in 1756 and died in 1820. He served as a Calvinistic Methodist minister in Denbigh, a market town in northeast Wales with a strong nonconformist heritage. His ministry fell in the decades of Methodist consolidation following the death of Howel Harris (1773) and Daniel Rowland (1790), when the movement was forming itself into a denomination.

## "Early to Bear the Yoke Excels"

This hymn addresses young people with a message drawn from the Lamentations of Jeremiah: "It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth." The Welsh evangelical tradition placed great emphasis on the conversion and dedication of the young, and Jones's hymn gives that emphasis a musical voice. To bear the yoke early is presented not as a burden but as an excellence --- the gift of a life directed from its beginning toward the right end.
