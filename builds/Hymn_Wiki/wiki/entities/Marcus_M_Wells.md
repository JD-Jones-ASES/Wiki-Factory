# Marcus M. Wells

Marcus M. Wells is another in the long tradition of American farmer-musicians --- men who composed hymn tunes between the seasons of agricultural work and whose music found its way into the hymnals of the nation.

## Life

Wells was born in 1815 and spent much of his life in Hartwick, New York, farming. He had musical training and composed sacred music, but was not a professional musician. He died in 1895 at the age of eighty.

## "Holy Spirit, Faithful Guide"

Wells composed the hymn tune to accompany his own text, "Holy Spirit, Faithful Guide," written "on a rainy afternoon" when farm work was impossible. According to the hymnal tradition, he wrote both words and music at a single sitting. The hymn asks for the Holy Spirit's guiding presence "ever near the Christian's side" --- a prayer for divine companionship through life's uncertainties.

The hymn appeared in mid-century collections and was reprinted in several evangelical and Sunday-school books. It is one of the more accessible specimens of the informal, devotional style that characterized much American hymn-writing of the period.
