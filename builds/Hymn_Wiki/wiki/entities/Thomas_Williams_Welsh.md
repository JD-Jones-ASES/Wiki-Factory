# Thomas Williams of Glamorganshire

Thomas Williams of Glamorganshire belongs to the second generation of Welsh Calvinistic Methodist hymnody --- the generation that inherited the revival fire of Williams of Pantycelyn and kept it burning through the turn of the century.

## Life

Williams was born in 1761 and died in 1844, living across eight decades of Welsh religious history. His long life spanned the height of the eighteenth-century revival, the quieter consolidation of Welsh Methodism, and the early decades of the nineteenth century.

## His Hymns

Williams wrote in the tradition of penitential and devotional hymnody that characterized the Calvinistic Methodist movement. His hymn "Unto Thy Presence Coming" approaches God with the awe and self-abasement that marked the Reformed Welsh sensibility: the soul arriving before the divine throne not in triumph but in dependence. "O Had I the Wings of a Dove" borrows the imagery of the Psalms to express the longing for God's presence.

Both hymns share the characteristic Welsh hymnic quality of dwelling in the emotional reality of faith --- not merely describing doctrinal positions but enacting the soul's movement toward God.
