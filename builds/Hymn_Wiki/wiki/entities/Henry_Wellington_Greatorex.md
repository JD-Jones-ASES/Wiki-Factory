# Henry Wellington Greatorex

Henry Wellington Greatorex was one of the most polished musicians in mid-nineteenth-century America --- an Englishman by birth and training who brought European cathedral standards to American church music.

## Life

Greatorex was born in 1813 in England. He came to America and established himself as an organist and music director in major eastern cities. He died in 1858 at the age of forty-five, leaving behind a body of work that influenced the choral standards of American churches for decades.

## Musical Work

His *Collection of Psalm and Hymn Tunes, Chants, and Anthems* (1851) was among the most carefully prepared and widely used American collections of its time. It aimed at a higher musical standard than most Sunday-school collections of the era, drawing on both European and American sources.

Greatorex is particularly remembered for his arrangements and compositions of chants and doxologies. His versions of the chant were used in many Episcopal and high-church Protestant congregations. His "Gloria Patri" chant setting was widely reprinted.

According to Metcalf, Greatorex's work helped elevate the musical standard of American Protestant worship toward European cathedral quality, a project that Lowell Mason had also championed. His early death cut short a career that showed great promise. (Metcalf, p. 256)
