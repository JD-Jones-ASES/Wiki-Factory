# Ithamar Conkey

Ithamar Conkey was an American bass singer who made a lasting contribution to congregational music through the composition of "Rathbun," the tune universally associated with [[Sir_John_Bowring]]'s "In the Cross of Christ I Glory."

## The Tune "Rathbun"

The tune "Rathbun" was composed by Conkey for Bowring's solemn hymn of personal consecration. Its strong, dignified movement suits the exalted simplicity of Bowring's imagery — the Cross "towering o'er the wrecks of time." The tune's name, "Rathbun," is presumably a place-name dedication in the common practice of American hymn-tune composers.

Conkey is one of those figures in hymn history whose entire reputation rests on a single contribution — yet what a contribution. The hymn and tune together have remained in constant use across denominations, carrying the Cross-centered devotion of Bowring's words into the singing life of the church.
