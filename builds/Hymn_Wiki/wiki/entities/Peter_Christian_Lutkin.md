# Peter Christian Lutkin

Peter Christian Lutkin (1858–1931) was an American musician, composer, and educator best known as the founding Dean of the School of Music at Northwestern University and as one of the music editors of the Methodist Hymnal of 1905.

## Life and Career

Lutkin was a native of Wisconsin. He received the degree of Doctor of Music from Syracuse University. He served as Dean of the School of Music at Northwestern University from 1897 onward, building it into a major center of music education. He was one of the two principal music editors of the 1905 Methodist Hymnal, collaborating with [[Karl_Pomeroy_Harrington]] of Wesleyan University.

## Tunes in the Methodist Hymnal

Lutkin contributed nineteen tunes to the Methodist Hymnal — the largest single contribution of any editor to that collection:

Copenhagen, Kolding, Kiel, Baptism, Prescott, Upham, Bellville, Joshua, St. Barbara, Gleason, Transfiguration, Racine, Caryl, Camp, Patten, Olivarius, Theodore, Lanier, and The Lord Bless You and Keep You.

"The Lord Bless You and Keep You" became one of Lutkin's most enduring compositions, widely used as a choral benediction.

## Methodist Hymnal Context (Nutter)

According to Nutter and Tillett, the selection of Lutkin and [[Karl_Pomeroy_Harrington]] as music editors brought professional academic musicianship to the compilation of the 1905 Hymnal. Lutkin's nineteen tunes constitute a significant body of original composition written specifically for the volume. The geographic names in many of his tunes (Copenhagen, Kolding, Kiel) reflect a Scandinavian flavor, while others (Racine, Evanston neighborhood tunes) reflect his Northwestern University context.
