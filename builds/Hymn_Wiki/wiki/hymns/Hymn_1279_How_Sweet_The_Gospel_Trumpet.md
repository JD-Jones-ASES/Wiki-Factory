## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 1279*

```
How sweet the gospel trumpet sounds!
    Its notes are grace and love;
  Its echo through the world resounds,
    From Jesus’ throne above.
```
