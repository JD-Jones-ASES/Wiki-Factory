## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 403*

```
  1 Amazing grace! (how sweet the sound!)
      That saved a wretch like me!
    I once was lost, but now am found;
      Was blind, but now I see.

  2 Through many dangers, toils, and snares,
      I have already come;
    ‘Tis grace has brought me safe thus far,
      And grace will lead me home.

  3 The Lord has promised good to me,
      His word my hope secures;
    He will my shield and portion be
      As long as life endures.

  4 Yes, when this heart and flesh shall fail,
      And mortal life shall cease,
    I shall possess within the vail
      A life of joy and peace.
```

*Note: Campbell’s 1870 edition printed four of Newton’s original six stanzas. The well-known second stanza ("’Twas grace that taught my heart to fear") was not included in this collection.*
