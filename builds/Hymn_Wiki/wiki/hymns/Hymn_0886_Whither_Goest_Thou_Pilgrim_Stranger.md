## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 886*

```
Whither goest thou, pilgrim stranger,
    Passing through this darksome vale?
  Knowest thou not ’tis full of danger,
    And will not thy courage fail?
```
