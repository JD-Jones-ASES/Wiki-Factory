## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 784*

```
Saviour! teach me, day by day,
  Love’s sweet lessons to obey;
  Sweeter lessons can not be,
  Loving him who first loved me.

  2 With a child-like heart of love,
  At thy bidding may I move;
  Prompt to serve and follow thee,
  Loving him who first loved me.

  3 Teach me all thy steps to trace,
  Strong to follow in thy grace;
  Learning how to love from thee,
  Loving him who first loved me.

  4 Love in loving finds employ—
  In obedience all her joy;
  Ever new that joy will be,
  Loving him who first loved me.

  5 Thus may I rejoice to show
  That I feel the love I owe;
  Singing, till thy face I see,
  Of his love who first loved me.
```

She is also the author of Paraphrases and Hymns for Congregational Singing, 1853.
