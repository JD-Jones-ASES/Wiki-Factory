## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 736*

```
To God, the Only Wise,
    Our Saviour and our King;
  Let all the saints below the skies
    Their humble praises bring.

  2 ’Tis his almighty love,
    His counsel and his care,
  Preserve us safe from sin and death,
    And every hurtful snare.

  3 He will present our souls,
    Unblemished and complete,
  Before the glory of his face,
    With joys divinely great.

  4 Then all the chosen seed
    Shall meet around the throne,
  Shall bless the conduct of his grace,
    And make his wonders known.

  5 To our Redeemer, God,
    Wisdom and power belong,
  Immortal crowns of majesty,
    And everlasting song.
```

## Historical Context

### From Duffield (1886)

In the Hymns, Book I., No. 51, this is entitled "Preserving Grace. — Jude 24, 25." It has five stanzas. It was first placed at the close of a sermon on this theme.
