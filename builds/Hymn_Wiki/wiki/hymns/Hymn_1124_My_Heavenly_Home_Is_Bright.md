## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 1124*

```
My heavenly home is bright and fair,
        We’ll be gathered home;
  Nor death nor sighing visit there,
        We’ll be gathered home:
```
