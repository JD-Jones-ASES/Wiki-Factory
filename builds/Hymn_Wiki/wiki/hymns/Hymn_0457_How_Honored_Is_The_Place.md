## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 457*

```
How honored is the place,
    Where we adoring stand!
  Zion, the glory of the earth,
    And beauty of the land.

  2 Bulwarks of grace defend
    The city where we dwell;
  While walls of strong salvation made,
    Defy th’ assaults of hell.

  3 Lift up th’ eternal gates,
    The doors wide open fling;
  Enter, ye nations, that obey
    The statutes of our King.

  4 Here taste unmingled joys,
    And live in perfect peace;
  You that have known Jehovah’s name,
    And ventured on his grace.

  5 Trust in the Lord, ye saints;
    And banish all your fears,
  Strength in the Lord Jehovah dwells,
    Eternal as his years.
```

### From Duffield (1886)

Samuel Stennett, is one of five which he contributed to Rippon's Selection, 1787, and is therefore so dated. General Sir Henry Havelock was accustomed to assemble his men for prayer in a chamber in one of the great pagodas in India, with idols on every side. It was at Rangoon, during the Burmese war of 1824, that his men consequently obtained the name of *' Havelock's saints." "Call out Havelock's saints," ordered Sir Archibald Campbell.
