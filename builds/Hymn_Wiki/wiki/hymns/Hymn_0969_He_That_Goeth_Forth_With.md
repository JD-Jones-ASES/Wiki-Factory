## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 969*

```
He that goeth forth with weeping,
    Bearing precious seed in love,
  Never tiring, never sleeping,
    Findeth mercy from above.

  2 Soft descend the dews of heaven;
    Bright the rays celestial shine;
  Precious fruits will thus be given,
    Through the influence all divine.

  3 Sow thy seed; be never weary;
    Let no fears thy soul annoy;
  Be the prospect ne’er so dreary,
    Thou shalt reap the fruits of joy.

  4 Lo! the scene of verdure brightening,
    In the rising grain appear;
  Look again; the fields are whitening,
    For the harvest time is near.
```

## Historical Context

### From Duffield (1886)

The date of this hymn is 1836, at which time Dr. Hastings pre- pared the Christian Psalmist. He had been assiduously laboring to improve the standard of current hymnody, and this hymn may be taken as a true expression of his own feelings in sowing pre- cious seed.
