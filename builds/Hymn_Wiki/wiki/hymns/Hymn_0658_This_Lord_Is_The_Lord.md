## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 658*

```
This Lord is the Lord we adore,
    Our faithful unchangeable Friend,
  Whose love is as large as his power,
    And neither knows measure nor end.

  2 ’Tis Jesus, the First and the Last,
    Whose Spirit shall guide us safe home;
  We’ll praise him for all that is past,
    And trust him for all that’s to come.
```

## Historical Context

### From Duffield (1886)

The title of the original hymn is, " If there arise among you a Prophet, or a Dreamer of Dreams, etc. — Deut. 13 : I," etc. In Hart' s Hymns, 1759 (2d ed., 1762) this is No. jt,. It has seven double stanzas and commences, " No prophet, no dreamer of dreams." The usual two-stanza hymn, with the above first line, has been formed from this longer piece by taking the last stanza and dividing it.
