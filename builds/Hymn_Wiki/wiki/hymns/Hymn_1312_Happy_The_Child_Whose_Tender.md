## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 1312*

```
Happy the child whose tender years
    Receive instruction well,
  Who hates the sinner’s path, and fears
    The road that leads to hell.

  2 ’Twill save us from a thousand snares
    To mind religion young,
  Grace will preserve our following years,
    And make our virtues strong.

  3 To thee, Almighty God, to thee
    Our childhood we resign;
  ’Twill please us to look back and see
    That our whole lives were thine.

  4 O let the work of prayer and praise
    Employ my youngest breath;
  Thus I’m prepared for longer days,
    Or fit for early death.
```
