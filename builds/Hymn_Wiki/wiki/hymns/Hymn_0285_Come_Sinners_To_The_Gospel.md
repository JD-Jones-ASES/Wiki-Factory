## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 285*

```
Come, sinners, to the gospel feast;
  O, do no longer stay;
  Let every soul be Jesus’ guest,
  O, do no longer stay away!
```

## Scripture References

- Luke 14:17.

## Historical Context

### From Nutter & Tillett (1911)

"The Great Supper'* is the title to this impressive hymn of invitation and wel- come to the sinner. It is based on Luke xiv. 16-24. It was first published in 1747 in the author's Hymns for Those That Seek and Those That Have Redemption in the Blood of Jesus Christ.
