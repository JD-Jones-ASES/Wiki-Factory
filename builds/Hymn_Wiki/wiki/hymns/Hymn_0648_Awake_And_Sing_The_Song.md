## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 648*

```
Awake, and sing the song
    Of Moses and the Lamb!
  Wake, every heart and every tongue,
    To praise the Saviour’s name!

  2 Sing of his dying love!
    Sing of his rising power!
  Sing how he intercedes above
    For those whose sins he bore!

  3 Sing on your heavenly way,
    You ransomed sinners, sing;
  Sing on, rejoicing every day
    In Christ, the glorious King.

  4 Soon shall you hear him say,
    “You blessed children, come,”
  Soon will he call you hence away,
    And take his pilgrims home.
```

## Historical Context

### From Duffield (1886)

William Hammond, in 1745, published a collection of original poems, entitled Psalms, Hymns and Spiritual Songs. The author's title was, "Before Singing of Hymns, by way of Introduction." Scriptural allusion is made to Rev. 15:3. From this the present hymn was taken.
