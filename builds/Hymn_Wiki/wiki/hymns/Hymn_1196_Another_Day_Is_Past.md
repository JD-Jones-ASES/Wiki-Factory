## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 1196*

```
Another day is past,
    The hours for ever fled;
  And time is bearing me away,
    To mingle with the dead.

  2 My mind in perfect peace
    My Father’s care shall keep;
  I yield to gentle slumber now,
    For thou canst never sleep.

  3 How blesséd, Lord, are they,
    On thee securely stayed!
  Nor shall they be in life alarmed,
    Nor be in death dismayed.
```

## Historical Context

### From Duffield (1886)

Among the many translations of the later Latin hymns executed by Isaac Williams, B. D., this may claim an honorable place. It bears a passing resemblance to the pseudo-Ambrosian hymn, Diei luce reddita, but it is scarcely close enough to be accepted as a ver- sion.
