## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 481*

```
Come, you that love the Lord indeed,
  Who are from sin and bondage freed,
  Submit to all the ways of God,
  And walk the narrow, happy road.
```
