## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 800*

```
My days are gliding swiftly by,
    And I a pilgrim stranger,
  Would not detain them as they fly—
    Those hours of toil and danger.
```
