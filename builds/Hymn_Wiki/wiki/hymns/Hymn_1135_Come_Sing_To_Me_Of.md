## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 1135*

```
Come, sing to me of heaven,
    When I’m about to die;
  Sing songs of holy ecstasy,
    To waft my soul on high.
```
