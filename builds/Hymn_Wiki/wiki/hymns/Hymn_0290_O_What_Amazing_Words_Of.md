## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 290*

```
O what amazing words of grace
    Are in the gospel found,
  Suited to every sinner’s case
    Who hears the joyful sound!

  2 Come, then, with all your wants and wounds
    Your every burden bring;
  Here love, unchanging love, abounds—
    A deep celestial spring.

  3 This spring with living water flows,
    And heavenly joy imparts;
  Come, thirsty souls! your wants disclose,
    And drink with thankful hearts.

  4 Millions of sinners, vile as you,
    Have here found life and peace;
  Come then, and prove its virtues too,
    And drink, adore, and bless.
```

## Historical Context

### From Nutter & Tillett (1911)

Salvation, like a river, rolls Abundant, free, and clear. 3 Come, then, with all your wants and wounds ; Your every burden bring: Here love, unchanging love, abounds, A deep, celestial spring. This is from a hymn of six stanzas which appeared in the first edition of the author's Hymns, 1789. The first and third stanzas here given are by Medley, but the second stanza is from some un- known hand.
