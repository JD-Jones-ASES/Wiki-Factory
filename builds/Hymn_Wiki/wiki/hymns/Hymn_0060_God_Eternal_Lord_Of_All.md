## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 60*

```
God eternal, Lord of all!
  Lowly at thy feet we fall:
  All the earth doth worship thee,
  We amid the throng would be.

  2 All the holy angels cry,
  Hail, thrice holy, God Most High,
  Glorified Apostles raise,
  Night and day, continual praise.
```

## Historical Context

### From Duffield (1886)

An original hymn, commencing, " God eternal, mighty King," was contributed, in 1848, by Dr. Millard, to The Devout Chorister. It is from that piece that this present hymn has been taken.
