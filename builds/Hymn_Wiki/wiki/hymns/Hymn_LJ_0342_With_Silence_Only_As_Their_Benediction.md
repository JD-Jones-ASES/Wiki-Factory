## Text

> *From [[Book_of_Hymns_for_Public_and_Private_Devotion|A Book of Hymns]] (1848), Hymn 342*

```
1 With silence only as their benediction,
          God's angels come
    Where, in the shadow of a great affliction,
          The soul sits dumb.
  2 Yet would we say, what every heart approveth,--
          Our Father's will,
    Calling to Him the dear ones whom he loveth,
          Is mercy still.
  3 Not upon us or ours the solemn angel
          Hath evil wrought;
    The funeral anthem is a glad evangel;
          The good die not!
  4 God calls our loved ones, but we lose not wholly
          What He has given;
    They live on earth in thought and deed, as truly
          As in His heaven.
```
