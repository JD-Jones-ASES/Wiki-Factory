## Text

> *From [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), Hymn 742*

```
Head of the Church triumphant!
    We joyfully adore thee;
  Till thou appear, thy members here
    Shall sing like those in glory.

  2 We lift our hearts and voices
    In blest anticipation,
  And cry aloud, and give to God
    The praise of our salvation.
```

## Historical Context

### From Duffield (1886)

This noble hymn of praise was composed by Charles Wesley, in 1745, and embraced in his Hymns for Thnes of Trouble and Perse- cution.
