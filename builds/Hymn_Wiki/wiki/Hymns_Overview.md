# Hymns

This wiki contains **1,324 hymns** from [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book|The Christian Hymn Book]] (1870), compiled by [[Alexander_Campbell|Alexander Campbell]] and revised by a committee of the Disciples of Christ.

Each hymn page includes the full text, meter marking, topic heading, author attribution, and any relevant Bible verse connections.

## Browse by Section

Campbell organized his hymnal into eleven sections:

| Section | Hymns | Theme |
|---------|-------|-------|
| I. The Holy Scriptures | 1-23 | God's Word |
| II. God | 24-116 | Being, Creation, Providence, Redemption |
| III. Christ | 117-265 | Nativity, Ministry, Crucifixion, Resurrection, Ascension |
| IV. The Gospel | 266-440 | Invitations, Faith, Baptism, Remission, Hope |
| V. The Church | 441-610 | Fellowship, Lord's Supper, Prayer, Growth |
| VI. Public Worship | 611-759 | Lord's Day, Praise, Opening, Closing |
| VII. The New Life | 760-1032 | Trust, Aspirations, Conflicts, Devotions |
| VIII. The Present and the Future | 1033-1169 | Death, Advent, Judgment, Heaven |
| IX. Home | 1170-1229 | Family, Morning, Evening, Youth and Age |
| X. Times and Seasons | 1230-1304 | Harvest, New Year, Missions, Sea, Marriage |
| XI. Miscellaneous | 1305-1331 | Various |

## Browse by Meter

- **Long Meter (L.M.)** --- 8.8.8.8 syllable pattern
- **Common Meter (C.M.)** --- 8.6.8.6 syllable pattern
- **Short Meter (S.M.)** --- 6.6.8.6 syllable pattern
- **Peculiar Meter (P.M.)** --- irregular patterns

## Find a Hymn
