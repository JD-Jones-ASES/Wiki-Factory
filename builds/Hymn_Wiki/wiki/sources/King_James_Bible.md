# King James Bible

The Authorized Version of the Bible, first published in 1611, commissioned by King James I of England. Used in this wiki as the reference text for all scripture connections.

## Role in This Wiki

The KJV serves as the **scripture reference layer** connecting hymns to their Biblical foundations. Over 450 hymns in [[The_Christian_Hymn_Book]] include explicit scripture references, and many more draw on Biblical language and themes without citation.

The relationship between the KJV and English-language hymnody is deep:

- **Psalm paraphrases** form an entire tradition of hymn writing, with [[Isaac_Watts]] being the pioneer of English metrical psalmody
- **KJV language** shaped how hymn writers expressed theological ideas --- phrases like "amazing grace," "rock of ages," and "nearer my God to thee" echo Biblical vocabulary
- **Biblical narratives** inspired countless hymns, from Christmas nativity hymns to Easter resurrection songs
- **Prophetic imagery** from Isaiah, Revelation, and the Psalms permeates hymn texts

## Most-Referenced Books

The books of the Bible most frequently referenced by hymns in this collection:

- **Psalms** --- by far the most referenced, the foundation of sung worship
- **Isaiah** --- prophetic imagery of redemption and glory
- **John** --- theology of divine love and eternal life
- **Matthew** --- narratives of Christ's life and teaching
- **Revelation** --- visions of heaven and the heavenly choir
- **Romans** --- theology of grace and justification
