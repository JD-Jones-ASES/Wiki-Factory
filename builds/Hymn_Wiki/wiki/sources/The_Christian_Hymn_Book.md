# The Christian Hymn Book

*A Compilation of Psalms, Hymns and Spiritual Songs, Original and Selected.* Compiled by [[Alexander_Campbell|Alexander Campbell]] and others. Revised and enlarged by a committee. Published in Cincinnati by H. S. Bosworth, 1870.

## Overview

This hymnal is the backbone of the Hymn Wiki. It contains **1,324 numbered hymns** organized into 11 sections covering the full range of Christian worship and devotion. The book was compiled for the Disciples of Christ (also known as the Christian Church or Churches of Christ), a movement that Alexander Campbell helped found in the early 19th century.

The original hymn book was Campbell's personal compilation. In 1864, he offered the copyright to the American Christian Missionary Society on two conditions: that a committee would revise and enlarge it, and that profits would go to the missionary society. The revision committee --- Isaac Errett, W. K. Pendleton, W. T. Moore, T. M. Allen, and A. S. Hayden --- completed their work in August 1865.

## Structure

The hymnal is organized into eleven sections:

| Section | Title | Hymns |
|---------|-------|-------|
| I | The Holy Scriptures | 1-23 |
| II | God (Being, Perfections, Creation, Providence, Redemption) | 24-116 |
| III | Christ (Nativity through Mediatorial Reign) | 117-265 |
| IV | The Gospel (Proclamation through Hope of Eternal Life) | 266-440 |
| V | The Church (Constitution, Fellowship, Lord's Supper, Prayer) | 441-610 |
| VI | Public Worship (Lord's Day, Praise, Opening, Closing) | 611-759 |
| VII | The New Life (Trust, Aspirations, Conflicts, Devotions) | 760-1032 |
| VIII | The Present and the Future (Death, Advent, Judgment, Heaven) | 1033-1169 |
| IX | Home (Family, Morning, Evening, Youth and Age) | 1170-1229 |
| X | Times and Seasons (Harvest, New Year, Missionary, Sea, Marriage) | 1230-1304 |
| XI | Miscellaneous | 1305-1331 |

## Key Features

- **Meter markings** for every hymn (L.M., C.M., S.M., etc.) enabling congregational singing
- **Numbered stanzas** for easy reference when selecting verses to sing
- **Subject headings** connecting each hymn to its theological topic
- **Scripture references** for many hymns, linking them to Bible passages
- **First-line index** for locating hymns by their opening words
- **Author attributions** for approximately 960 of the 1,324 hymns

## Most-Represented Authors

The hymnal draws heavily on the great English hymn writers:

- [[Isaac Watts]] --- the most frequently represented author
- [[Charles Wesley]] --- extensive Methodist contribution
- [[Philip Doddridge]] --- devotional and evangelical hymns
- [[William Cowper]] --- hymns of spiritual struggle and faith
- [[John Newton]] --- the Olney Hymns tradition
- [[James Montgomery]] --- lyrical and missionary hymns
- [[Thomas Hastings]] --- American hymn writer

## Significance

This hymnal represents the Restoration Movement's approach to congregational singing: drawing from the best of English Protestant hymnody while organizing material for practical worship use. Its comprehensive scope --- from scripture and doctrine through personal devotion to civic occasions --- made it a complete resource for church and home.
