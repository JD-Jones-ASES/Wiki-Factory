# The Hymns and Hymn Writers of the Church

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Authors** | Charles S. Nutter, D.D. and Wilbur F. Tillett, D.D., LL.D. (Dean, Vanderbilt University) |
| **Published** | 1911, New York: Eaton & Mains; Cincinnati: Jennings & Graham |
| **Type** | Annotated hymnal with biographical reference |
| **Scope** | 383 hymns of the 1905 Methodist Hymnal, plus comprehensive biographical indexes |
| **Perspective** | Methodist; scholarly and pastoral |

## Overview

Nutter and Tillett's work is the most detailed annotated hymnal in our source collection. Every one of the 383 hymns in the 1905 Methodist Hymnal receives full text plus scholarly annotation following a systematic ten-point method. The biographical indexes provide profiles of ~306 hymn authors/translators and all tune composers represented in the hymnal.

## Structure

**Annotated Hymns** (organized by the Hymnal's topical scheme):
- Worship (hymns 1–42)
- The Trinity (43–45)
- The Father (46–60)
- The Son (61–102)
- The Holy Spirit (103–109)
- The Holy Scriptures (110–113)
- Institutions of Christianity (114–128)
- The Gospel Call (129–159)
- The Christian Life (160–296)
- Time and Eternity (297–330)
- Special Subjects and Occasions (331–372)
- Doxologies (373–374)
- Chants and Occasional Pieces (375–383)

**Reference sections:**
- Biographical Index of Authors (~306 entries)
- Biographical Index of Composers of Tunes
- Bibliography of Hymnology
- Five indexes (Subjects, Scripture Texts, Tunes, Stanza First Lines, Hymn First Lines)
- The Psalter (responsive readings arranged by Sunday)
- The Ritual (Baptism, Lord's Supper, Matrimony, Burial)

## Distinctive Contribution

The ten-point annotation method is unmatched for rigor. Nutter and Tillett systematically document original publication sources, textual alterations (who changed which lines, when, why), omitted stanzas, scripture bases, and — uniquely — actively debunk hymn myths and legends. The biographical indexes provide the most comprehensive single-source collection of hymn author profiles in our collection, covering writers from all denominations. The Scripture Texts index maps hymns to Bible passages, complementing our existing `_scripture_index.md`.

## Source File

`raw/Nutter.txt` — 90,912 lines, OCR scan. Quality: generally good. Known degraded pages (smudged/damaged originals) around the Gilder annotation. Decorative Gothic typeface headers consistently misread by OCR. The Psalter section (~14,000 lines) is biblical text in responsive format. Main hymn annotations and biographical indexes are clean and usable.
