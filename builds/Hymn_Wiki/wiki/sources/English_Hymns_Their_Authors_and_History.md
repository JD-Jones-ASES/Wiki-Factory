## Overview

Duffield's *English Hymns* is a reference encyclopedia organized alphabetically by the first line of each hymn. Based on Dr. Charles Seymour Robinson's hymnal *Laudes Domini*, each entry provides a self-contained scholarly essay covering authorship, circumstances of composition, textual variants, reception history, and vivid anecdotes of use.

## Structure

The book has no chapters. It is a single alphabetical sequence from "A broken heart, my God, my King" (Watts) through "Zion, the marvellous story be telling" (Muhlenberg). Back matter includes:
- Chronological Table of English Hymn-Writers (1400–1886)
- Chronological Table of American Hymn-Writers (1639–1886)
- Index of Authors
- Index of Hymns Annotated or Mentioned
- Topical Index

## Distinctive Contribution

Duffield's alphabetical-by-first-line organization makes this the most directly useful source for enriching individual hymn pages. Where Ryden and Butterworth-Brown organize by author or theme, Duffield's entries map one-to-one to specific hymns. His treatment of textual history — who altered what line, when, and why — is more detailed than any other source in this wiki. The anecdotes of how hymns were used in extremis (battlefields, deathbeds, revivals) are particularly vivid. This source also covers German hymns in English translation and Latin hymns in English translation, cross-referencing his companion volume *The Latin Hymn-Writers and Their Hymns*.
