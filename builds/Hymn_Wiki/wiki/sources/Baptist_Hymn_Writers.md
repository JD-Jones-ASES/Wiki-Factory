## Overview

Henry Burrage's work is the most comprehensive survey of Baptist contributions to hymnody ever compiled. Organized by nationality and then chronologically within each national section, it provides individual biographical sketches of over 200 hymn writers, with attention to the circumstances of composition for their most famous hymns.

## Structure

- **Anabaptist Hymn Writers** — Felix Mantz, Michael Sattler, and other 16th-century martyrs
- **English Baptist Hymn Writers** — from John Bunyan and Benjamin Keach through Charles Spurgeon (~100 writers)
- **American Baptist Hymn Writers** — from Benjamin Cleavland through Arthur S. Phelps (~100 writers)
- **German Baptist Hymn Writers** — Lehmann, Kobner, Rauschenbusch, and others
- **International sections** — Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, French, Welsh, Spanish, Mexican, Greek, Indian, Burmese, Assamese, Chinese, Japanese, and African Baptist writers
- **Appendices** — Catalogs of Baptist hymn books (English, American, German)

## Distinctive Contribution

Burrage provides the deepest available treatment of Baptist-tradition hymn writers, many of whom appear nowhere else in our source collection. His coverage of 16th-century Anabaptist hymn-martyrs, global mission-field hymnody, and minor English/American Baptist writers fills significant gaps. Major figures like [[Robert_Robinson]], [[Samuel_Medley]], [[John_Fawcett]], [[Edward_Mote]], and [[Samuel_Francis_Smith]] receive extended treatment from a specifically Baptist perspective. The book does NOT cover non-Baptist writers (Watts, Newton, Cowper, Fanny Crosby) in their own right.
