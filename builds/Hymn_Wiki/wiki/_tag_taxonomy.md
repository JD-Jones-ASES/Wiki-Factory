# Tag Taxonomy

Controlled vocabulary for the Hymn Wiki. All tags must appear here before use. Propose new tags by adding them to the appropriate category.

## Eras

- `#era-early-church` --- 1st-6th century (apostolic and patristic)
- `#era-medieval` --- 7th-15th century (monastic and scholastic)
- `#era-reformation` --- 16th century (Luther, Calvin, and contemporaries)
- `#era-17th-century` --- 17th century (post-Reformation, Keach, Bunyan, early Baptist/Puritan)
- `#era-post-reformation` --- 17th century (Gerhardt, Pietism's roots)
- `#era-18th-century` --- Watts, Wesley, Great Awakening
- `#era-19th-century` --- Golden age of English and American hymnody
- `#era-20th-century` --- Modern hymn writing (through 1930 in our sources)

## Denominational Traditions

- `#tradition-lutheran` --- German and Scandinavian Lutheran
- `#tradition-methodist` --- Wesleyan tradition
- `#tradition-calvinist` --- Reformed, Presbyterian
- `#tradition-catholic` --- Roman Catholic
- `#tradition-anglican` --- Church of England, Episcopal
- `#tradition-baptist` --- Baptist congregations
- `#tradition-moravian` --- Zinzendorf and the Moravian Brethren
- `#tradition-congregational` --- Congregationalist / Independent
- `#tradition-presbyterian` --- Scottish and American Presbyterian
- `#tradition-unitarian` --- Unitarian writers
- `#tradition-quaker` --- Society of Friends

## Musical Forms

- `#meter-common` --- C.M. (Common Meter: 8.6.8.6)
- `#meter-long` --- L.M. (Long Meter: 8.8.8.8)
- `#meter-short` --- S.M. (Short Meter: 6.6.8.6)
- `#meter-common-double` --- C.M.D. (Common Meter Double)
- `#meter-long-particular` --- L.P.M. (Long Particular Meter)
- `#meter-short-particular` --- S.P.M. (Short Particular Meter)
- `#meter-peculiar` --- P.M. (Peculiar Meter / irregular)
- `#meter-sevens` --- 7s (seven-syllable lines)
- `#meter-eights` --- 8s (eight-syllable lines)

## Roles

- `#composer` --- Primarily known as a tune/music composer rather than text writer
- `#hymn-writer` --- Primarily known as a hymn text writer
- `#poet` --- Poet who also wrote hymns
- `#author` --- Writer/author who contributed hymns
- `#reference` --- Reference work or source material

## Page Meta

- `#needs-review` --- Content needs verification or updating
- `#needs-expansion` --- Stub or thin page that should be enriched
- `#contradicted` --- Sources disagree; both accounts noted
- `#key-hymn` --- Historically significant or widely known hymn
- `#key-figure` --- Major figure in hymnody
- `#translated` --- Hymn translated from another language
