# Pythagorean Theorem

> Stub — expanded in Phase 2 with content from the ingested source books.

**Statement:** In a right triangle with legs of length $a$ and $b$ and hypotenuse of length $c$:

$$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$$

The theorem connects arithmetic with geometry in one of mathematics' oldest known results. It underlies the **distance formula** in the coordinate plane, which in turn is where the equation of a [[Circles|circle]] comes from.
