# Middle School Math

**Grades 6-8. The foundations that everything else is built on.**

This is where arithmetic starts to become algebra. You will work with integers and the number line, learn how fractions and decimals fit together, master ratios and percents, meet variables and expressions for the first time, and pick up the geometry and data-analysis habits that high school math assumes you already have.

---

## What You'll Learn

- **Numbers and operations** --- integers, fractions, decimals, and how to compute with all three cleanly.
- **Ratios, rates, percents** --- the bridge from arithmetic to algebra.
- **Expressions and variables** --- reading, writing, and evaluating algebraic expressions.
- **Coordinate plane and inequalities** --- plotting points, graphing simple lines, writing and solving one- and two-step inequalities.
- **Geometry foundations** --- perimeter, area, volume, the Pythagorean theorem, angle relationships, similar triangles.
- **Data and probability** --- mean, median, mode, range, histograms, and simple probability.
