# Pre-Calculus Overview

> [[_overview|Home]] > Pre-Calculus

Pre-Calculus is the bridge between algebra/trigonometry and calculus. You'll sharpen your fluency with functions, meet new ones (logarithms, exponentials, conic sections), and start thinking in terms of **rates, limits, and infinite processes** — the ideas calculus will formalize.

---

## What You'll Learn

- **Functions revisited:** domain, range, composition, inverse, transformations
- **Exponential functions:** growth, decay, the number _e_
- **Logarithms:** properties, equations, applications
- **Sequences and series:** arithmetic, geometric, summation, infinite series
- **Conic sections:** parabolas, ellipses, hyperbolas, circles (analytic approach)
- **Vectors and matrices:** basic operations and geometric interpretations
- **Complex numbers:** arithmetic, polar form, De Moivre's theorem
- **Pre-calculus limits:** the intuition of approaching a value
