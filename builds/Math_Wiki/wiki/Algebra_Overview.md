# Algebra Overview

> [[_overview|Home]] > Algebra

Algebra is the language of mathematics. It teaches you how to describe unknown quantities with symbols and then manipulate those symbols to answer questions. If arithmetic is about finding the answer, algebra is about finding the rule.

---

## What You'll Learn

- **Pre-Algebra:** integers, fractions, order of operations, ratios, percentages
- **Algebra 1:** expressions, linear equations and inequalities, graphing, systems, polynomials, factoring, quadratic basics
- **Algebra 2:** functions, transformations, polynomials beyond quadratics, rational expressions, exponentials, logarithms, sequences
