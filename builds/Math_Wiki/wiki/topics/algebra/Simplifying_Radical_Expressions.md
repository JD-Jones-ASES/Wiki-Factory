## Definitions

- **Square Root** --- If a 0, the principal square root of a, written a, is the non-negative number b such that b^2 = a. In other words, \[ a = b means b 0 and b^2 = a. \] For example, 25 = 5 because 5^2 = 25 and 5 0.
- **Radical Expression** --- A radical expression is an expression that contains a radical sign {x}. The expression under the radical sign is called the radicand. In 3x + 7, the radicand is 3x + 7.

## Key Properties

- **Product Property of Square Roots** --- If a 0 and b 0, then \[ ab = a b. \]
- **Quotient Property of Square Roots** --- If a 0 and b > 0, then \[ a/b = {a}{b}. \]
