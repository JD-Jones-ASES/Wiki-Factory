## Definitions

- **(untitled)** --- {Point-Slope Form}{ch02:point-slope} The equation of a line with slope m passing through the point (x_1, y_1) is \[ y - y_1 = m(x - x_1). \]
- **(untitled)** --- {Slope-Intercept Form}{ch02:slope-intercept} The equation of a line with slope m and y-intercept b is \[ y = mx + b. \] This is point-slope form applied to the point (0, b).
- **(untitled)** --- {Standard Form}{ch02:standard-form} The standard form of a linear equation is \[ Ax + By = C, \] where A, B, and C are integers with A 0 and (A, B, C) = 1 (no common factor).
- **(untitled)** --- A linear function is a function of the form \[ f(x) = mx + b,\] where m and b are real numbers with m 0. The domain of a linear function is (-, ).
- **(untitled)** --- A constant function is a function of the form \[ f(x) = b,\] where b is real number. The domain of a constant function is (-, ).
