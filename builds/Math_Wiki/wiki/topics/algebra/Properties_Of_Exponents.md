## Key Properties

- **Product of Powers** --- For every nonzero real number a and positive integers m and n: \[ a^m a^n = a^{m+n} \] To multiply powers with the same base, keep the base and add the exponents.
- **Power of a Power** --- For every nonzero real number a and positive integers m and n: \[ (a^m)^n = a^{mn} \] To raise a power to a power, keep the base and multiply the exponents.
- **Power of a Product** --- For all nonzero real numbers a and b and every positive integer n: \[ (ab)^n = a^n b^n \] To raise a product to a power, raise each factor to that power.
