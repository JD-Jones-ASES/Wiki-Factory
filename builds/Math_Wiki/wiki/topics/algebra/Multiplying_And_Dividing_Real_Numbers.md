# Multiplying and Dividing Real Numbers

## Key Properties

- **Multiplying Signed Numbers** --- itemize[nosep] The product of two numbers with the same sign is positive. The product of two numbers with different signs is negative. itemize \[ (+)(+) = + (-\,)(-\,) = + (+)(-\,) = - (-\,)(+) = - \]
- **Dividing Signed Numbers** --- The sign rules for division are the same as for multiplication: itemize[nosep] Same signs positive quotient. Different signs negative quotient. itemize
- **Multiplication by Zero and Division by Zero** --- For every real number a: itemize[nosep] a 0 = 0 0{a} = 0 (for a 0) a{0} is undefined --- division by zero is never allowed. itemize
