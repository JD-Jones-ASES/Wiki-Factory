## Definitions

- **Parabola** --- A parabola is the U-shaped curve that is the graph of a quadratic function f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c (with a 0). Every parabola is symmetric about a vertical line called its axis of symmetry.
- **Vertex** --- The vertex of a parabola is the point where the curve changes direction. It is the lowest point if the parabola opens upward, or the highest point if it opens downward.

## Key Properties

- **Axis of Symmetry and Vertex** --- For f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c (with a 0): itemize[nosep] The axis of symmetry is the vertical line x = -b{2a}. The vertex is the point (-b{2a},\; f\!(-b{2a})). itemize
