# Adding and Subtracting Real Numbers

## Key Properties

- **Adding Numbers with the Same Sign** --- To add two numbers with the same sign, add their absolute values and keep the common sign. \[ 3 + 5 = 8 (-3) + (-5) = -8 \]
- **Adding Numbers with Different Signs** --- To add two numbers with different signs, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger and keep the sign of the number with the larger absolute value. \[ (-7) + 4 = -3 9 + (-2) = 7 \]
- **Subtraction Rule** --- To subtract a number, add its opposite: \[ a - b = a + (-b) \]
