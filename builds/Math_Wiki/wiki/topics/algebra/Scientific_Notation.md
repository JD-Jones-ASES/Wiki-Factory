# Scientific Notation

## Definitions

- **Scientific Notation** --- A number is in scientific notation when it is written in the form \[ a 10^n \] where 1 a < 10 and n is an integer. The factor a is called the coefficient, and 10^n is the power of 10.
- **Scientific Notation** --- A number is in scientific notation when it is written in the form \[ a 10^n, \] where 1 a < 10 (so a has exactly one nonzero digit before the decimal point) and n is an integer.
- **Order of Magnitude** --- The order of magnitude of a number is the power of 10 closest to that number. Two quantities differ by one order of magnitude if one is roughly 10 times the other.
