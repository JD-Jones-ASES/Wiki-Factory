# The Real Numbers

## Definitions

- **Natural Numbers** --- The natural numbers (or counting numbers) are \[ = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, \}. \]
- **Whole Numbers** --- The whole numbers are the natural numbers together with zero: \{0, 1, 2, 3, \}.
- **Integers** --- The integers include all whole numbers and their opposites: \[ = \{, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, \}. \]

- **Irrational Numbers** --- An irrational number is a real number that cannot be written as a fraction of integers. Its decimal form neither terminates nor repeats. Examples: 2 1.41421, 3.14159

## Key Properties

- **Subset Relationships** --- The number sets are nested: \[ \] Every natural number is an integer, every integer is rational, and every rational number is real. Irrational numbers fill the remaining points on the number line.
