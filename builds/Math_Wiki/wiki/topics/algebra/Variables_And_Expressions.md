# Variables and Expressions

## Definitions

- **Variable** --- A variable is a letter or symbol that represents one or more numbers. Common choices are x, y, n, and t.
- **Constant** --- A constant is a quantity whose value does not change. The numbers 3, -7, 1/2, and are all constants.
- **Algebraic Expression** --- An algebraic expression is a combination of variables, constants, and operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and exponentiation). Examples: 3x + 5, 2a^2 - 7b, n{4} + 1
