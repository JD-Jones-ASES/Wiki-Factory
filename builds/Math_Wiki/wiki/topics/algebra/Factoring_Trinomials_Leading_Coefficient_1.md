# Factoring Trinomials: Leading Coefficient 1

## Key Properties

- **Factoring x^2 + bx + c** --- To factor x^2 + bx + c, look for two integers p and q such that \[ p + q = b and p q = c. \] If such integers exist, then \[ x^2 + bx + c = (x + p)(x + q). \]
