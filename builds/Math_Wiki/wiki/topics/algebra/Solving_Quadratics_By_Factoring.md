## Definitions

- **Quadratic Equation** --- A quadratic equation is an equation that can be written in the standard form \[ ax^2 + bx + c = 0, \] where a, b, and c are real numbers and a 0. The highest power of the variable is 2.

## Key Properties

- **Zero Product Property** --- If a and b are real numbers and \[ ab = 0, \] then a = 0 or b = 0 (or both). In words: if the product of two factors is zero, then at least one of the factors must be zero.
