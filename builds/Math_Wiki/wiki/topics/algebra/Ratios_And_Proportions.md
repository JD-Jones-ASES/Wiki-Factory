# Ratios and Proportions

## Definitions

- **Ratio** --- A ratio is a comparison of two quantities by division. The ratio of a to b can be written as \[ a/b, a : b, or a to b, \] where b 0.
- **Proportion** --- A proportion is an equation stating that two ratios are equal: \[ a/b = c/d, b 0,\; d 0. \]

## Key Properties

- **Cross-Multiplication Property** --- If a{b} = c{d} and b 0, d 0, then \[ ad = bc. \]
