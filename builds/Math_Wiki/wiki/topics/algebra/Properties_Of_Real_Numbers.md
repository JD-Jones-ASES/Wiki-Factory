## Key Properties

- **Commutative Properties** --- For all real numbers a and b: align* a + b &= b + a && (Commutative Property of Addition) \\ a b &= b a && (Commutative Property of Multiplication) align*
- **Associative Properties** --- For all real numbers a, b, and c: align* (a + b) + c &= a + (b + c) && (Associative Property of Addition) \\ (a b) c &= a (b c) && (Associative Property of Multiplication) align*
- **Identity Properties** --- For every real number a: align* a + 0 &= a && (Additive Identity: 0 is the identity) \\ a 1 &= a && (Multiplicative Identity: 1 is the identity) align*
- **Inverse Properties** --- For every real number a: align* a + (-a) &= 0 && (Additive Inverse: -a is the opposite of a) \\ a 1/a &= 1, a 0 && (Multiplicative Inverse: 1/a is the reciprocal) align*
- **Distributive Property** --- For all real numbers a, b, and c: \[ a(b + c) = ab + ac and a(b - c) = ab - ac \]
