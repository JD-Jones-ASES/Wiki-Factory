## Key Properties

- **Clearing Fractions with the LCD** --- To clear fractions from an equation, multiply every term on both sides by the least common denominator (LCD) of all the fractions.
- **Clearing Decimals** --- To clear decimals from an equation, multiply every term on both sides by the smallest power of~10 that makes all coefficients integers.
