# Percent Increase and Decrease

## Definitions

- **Percent Increase** --- A percent increase describes how much a quantity has gone up, expressed as a percent of the original amount.
- **Percent Decrease** --- A percent decrease describes how much a quantity has gone down, expressed as a percent of the original amount.
