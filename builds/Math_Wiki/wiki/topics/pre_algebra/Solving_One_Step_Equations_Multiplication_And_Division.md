## Key Properties

- **Multiplication Property of Equality** --- If a = b, then ac = bc (for any number~c). You can multiply both sides of an equation by the same number.
- **Division Property of Equality** --- If a = b and c 0, then a{c} = b{c}. You can divide both sides of an equation by the same nonzero number.
