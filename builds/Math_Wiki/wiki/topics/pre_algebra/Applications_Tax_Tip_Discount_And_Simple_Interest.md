- **Sales Tax** --- Sales tax is an extra amount of money charged by the government on purchases. It is calculated as a percent of the purchase price. \[ tax amount = tax rate price \]

- **Discount** --- A discount is a reduction in the original price of an item. \[ discount amount = discount rate original price \] \[ sale price = original price - discount amount \]
