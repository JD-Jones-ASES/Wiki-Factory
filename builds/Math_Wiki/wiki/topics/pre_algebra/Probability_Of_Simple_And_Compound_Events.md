## Definitions

- **Probability of an Event** --- If all outcomes are equally likely, the probability of event E is \[ P(E) = number of favorable outcomes {total number of outcomes}. \] Probability is always between 0 and 1 (inclusive): 0 P(E) 1.
- **Independent Events** --- Two events are independent if the outcome of one does not affect the outcome of the other. For example, flipping a coin and rolling a die are independent events.

## Key Properties

- **Complement Rule** --- The probability that event E does not occur is \[ P(not E) = 1 - P(E). \]
- **Multiplication Rule for Independent Events** --- If A and B are independent events, then \[ P(A and B) = P(A) P(B). \]
