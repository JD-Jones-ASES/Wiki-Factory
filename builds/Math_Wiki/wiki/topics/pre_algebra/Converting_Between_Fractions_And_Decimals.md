## Definitions

- **Terminating Decimal** --- A terminating decimal is a decimal that ends (has a finite number of digits after the decimal point). For example, 3{8} = 0.375.
- **Repeating Decimal** --- A repeating decimal is a decimal in which one or more digits repeat endlessly. A bar is placed over the repeating digits. For example, 1{3} = 0.333 = 0.3.

## Key Properties

- **Converting a Fraction to a Decimal** --- To convert a fraction to a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator: \[ a/b = a b \] The result is either a terminating decimal or a repeating decimal.
