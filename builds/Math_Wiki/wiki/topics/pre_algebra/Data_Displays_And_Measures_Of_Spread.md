## Definitions

- **Range** --- The range of a data set is the difference between the greatest and least values: \[ range = maximum - minimum. \]
- **Histogram** --- A histogram is a bar graph that shows how data are distributed across intervals (called bins). The bars touch because the intervals are consecutive. The height of each bar shows the frequency (count) ...
- **Five-Number Summary** --- The five-number summary of a data set consists of: itemize[nosep] Minimum (least value) Q_1 (first quartile---median of the lower half) Median (Q_2) Q_3 (third quartile---median of the upper half) Max...
- **Box Plot** --- A box plot (box-and-whisker plot) displays the five-number summary. The ``box'' spans from Q_1 to Q_3, a line inside marks the median, and ``whiskers'' extend to the minimum and maximum.

## Key Properties

- **Informal Outlier Rule** --- A data value is informally considered an outlier if it is more than 1.5 IQR below Q_1 or above Q_3.
