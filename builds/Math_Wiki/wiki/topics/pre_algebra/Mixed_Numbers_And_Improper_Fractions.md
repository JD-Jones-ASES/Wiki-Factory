## Definitions

- **Proper Fraction** --- A proper fraction has a numerator that is less than its denominator. Its value is between 0 and 1. itemize[nosep] 3{5},\; 7{8},\; 1{100} are proper fractions. itemize
- **Improper Fraction** --- An improper fraction has a numerator that is greater than or equal to its denominator. Its value is 1 or greater. itemize[nosep] 7{4},\; 11{3},\; 5{5} are improper fractions. itemize
- **Mixed Number** --- A mixed number combines a whole-number part and a proper-fraction part. itemize[nosep] 2\,3{4} means 2 + 3{4}. 5\,1{2} means 5 + 1{2}. itemize
