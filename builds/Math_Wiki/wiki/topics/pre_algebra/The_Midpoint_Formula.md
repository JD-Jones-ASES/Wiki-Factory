## Key Properties

- **The Midpoint Formula** --- The midpoint M of the segment with endpoints (x_1, y_1) and (x_2, y_2) is \[ M = (x_1 + x_2/2,\; y_1 + y_2/2). \] In words: average the x-coordinates and average the y-coordinates.
