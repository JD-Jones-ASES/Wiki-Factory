## Definitions

- **Coordinate Plane** --- The coordinate plane is formed by two perpendicular number lines called the x-axis (horizontal) and the y-axis (vertical). They intersect at the origin, labeled O = (0, 0).
- **Ordered Pair** --- An ordered pair (x, y) gives the coordinates of a point. The first number x tells how far to move left or right from the origin; the second number y tells how far to move up or down.

## Key Properties

- **Reflections in the Coordinate Plane** --- itemize[nosep] Reflecting (a, b) over the x-axis gives (a, -b). Reflecting (a, b) over the y-axis gives (-a, b). Reflecting (a, b) over the origin gives (-a, -b). itemize
