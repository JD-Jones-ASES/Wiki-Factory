# Proportions in Similar Figures

## Definitions

- **Scale Factor** --- The scale factor of two similar figures is the ratio of any pair of corresponding side lengths. If ABC DEF with AB = 6 and DE = 9, the scale factor from ABC to DEF is 6{9} = 2{3}.
