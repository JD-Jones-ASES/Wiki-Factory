# Product, Power, and Quotient Rules

## Key Properties

- **Product Rule for Exponents** --- For any nonzero base a and positive integers m and n, \[ a^m a^n = a^{m+n}. \] Keep the base and add the exponents.
- **Power Rule for Exponents** --- For any nonzero base a and positive integers m and n, \[ (a^m)^n = a^{mn}. \] Keep the base and multiply the exponents.
- **Power of a Product** --- For any nonzero bases a and b and positive integer n, \[ (ab)^n = a^n b^n. \] Distribute the exponent to each factor.
- **Quotient Rule for Exponents** --- For any nonzero base a and positive integers m and n with m > n, \[ a^m/a^n = a^{m-n}. \] Keep the base and subtract the exponents.
