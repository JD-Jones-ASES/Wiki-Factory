## Definitions

- **Rate** --- A rate is a ratio that compares two quantities measured in different units. itemize[nosep] 120 miles in 2 hours \7.50 for 3$ pounds itemize
- **Unit Rate** --- A unit rate is a rate in which the second quantity (the denominator) is~1. itemize[nosep] 60 miles per 1 hour = 60 mph \2.50 per 1$ pound itemize

## Key Properties

- **Finding a Unit Rate** --- To find a unit rate, divide both quantities by the denominator quantity so that the second quantity becomes~1. \[ a units/b units = a b/b b = a b/1 \]
