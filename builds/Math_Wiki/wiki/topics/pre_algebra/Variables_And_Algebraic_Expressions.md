## Definitions

- **Variable** --- A variable is a letter or symbol that represents an unknown or changeable value. Common choices are x, y, n, and t.
- **Algebraic Expression** --- An algebraic expression is a combination of numbers, variables, and operations (such as +, -, , ). Examples: 3x + 5, \; n{4} - 2, \; 7y.
- **Term** --- A term is a single number, a single variable, or a number multiplied by one or more variables. Terms in an expression are separated by + or - signs. In the expression 5x + 3, the terms are 5x and 3.

- **Constant** --- A constant is a term that has no variable. It is ``constant'' because its value never changes. In 5x + 3, the constant is~3.
