## Key Properties

- **Types of Angles** --- itemize[nosep] Acute: 0 < m < 90. Right: m = 90 (marked with a small square). Obtuse: 90 < m < 180. Straight: m = 180 (a straight line). itemize
- **Complementary Angles** --- Two angles are complementary if the sum of their measures is 90.
- **Supplementary Angles** --- Two angles are supplementary if the sum of their measures is 180.
- **Vertical Angles** --- When two lines intersect, the opposite angles are called vertical angles. Vertical angles are always equal in measure.
