## Definitions

- **Hypotenuse and Legs** --- In a right triangle, the side opposite the right angle is the hypotenuse---it is always the longest side. The other two sides are called legs.
- **Simplified Radical Form** --- A square root n is in simplified radical form when no perfect-square factor (other than~1) remains under the radical. For example, 50 = 25 2 = 52.
- **Pythagorean Triple** --- A Pythagorean triple is a set of three positive integers a, b, c with a^2 + b^2 = c^2.

## Key Properties

- **The Pythagorean Theorem** --- In a right triangle with legs a and b and hypotenuse c, \[ a^2 + b^2 = c^2. \]
- **Converse of the Pythagorean Theorem** --- If a^2 + b^2 = c^2 for the three sides of a triangle (where c is the longest side), then the triangle is a right triangle.
