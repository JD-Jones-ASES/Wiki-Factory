## Definitions

- **Linear Equation in Two Variables** --- A linear equation in two variables is any equation that can be written in the form y = mx + b, where m and b are real numbers. Its graph is always a straight line.
- **y-Intercept** --- The y-intercept of a line is the point where the line crosses the y-axis. At this point, x = 0. In the equation y = mx + b, the y-intercept is the point (0, b).

## Key Properties

- **Horizontal and Vertical Lines** --- itemize[nosep] The equation y = c (where c is a constant) graphs as a horizontal line through (0, c). The equation x = c graphs as a vertical line through (c, 0). itemize
