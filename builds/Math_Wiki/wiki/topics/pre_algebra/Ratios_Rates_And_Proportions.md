## Definitions

- **Ratio** --- A ratio is a comparison of two quantities by division. The ratio of a to b can be written as a{b}, as a : b, or as ``a to b,'' provided b 0.
- **Rate and Unit Rate** --- A rate is a ratio that compares two quantities measured in different units. A unit rate has a denominator of~1. For example, traveling 120 miles in 2 hours gives a unit rate of 120 mi{2 h} = 60 mi/h.
- **Proportion** --- A proportion is an equation that states two ratios are equal: \[ a/b = c/d, b 0,\; d 0. \]

## Key Properties

- **Cross Products** --- If a{b} = c{d} (with b 0 and d 0), then ad = bc. This property is called cross multiplication.
