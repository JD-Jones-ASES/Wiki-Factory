# Adding and Subtracting Integers

## Key Properties

- **Adding Integers---Same Sign** --- If two integers have the same sign: enumerate[nosep] Add their absolute values. Give the result the shared sign. enumerate Examples:\; 5 + 8 = 13; (-4) + (-6) = -10.

- **Subtracting Integers** --- To subtract an integer, add its opposite: \[ a - b = a + (-b). \] Example:\; 6 - 9 = 6 + (-9) = -3.
