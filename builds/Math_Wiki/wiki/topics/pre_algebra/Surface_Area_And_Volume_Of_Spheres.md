# Surface Area and Volume of Spheres

## Definitions

- **Sphere** --- A sphere is the set of all points in space that are a fixed distance (the radius) from a central point (the center).

## Key Properties

- **Surface Area of a Sphere** --- For a sphere with radius~r: \[ SA = 4 r^2 \]
- **Volume of a Sphere** --- For a sphere with radius~r: \[ V = 4{3} r^3 \]
