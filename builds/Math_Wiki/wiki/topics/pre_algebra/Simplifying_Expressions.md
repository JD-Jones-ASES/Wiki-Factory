# Simplifying Expressions

## Definitions

- **Term, Coefficient, and Constant** --- A term is a product of a number and one or more variables. The number part is the coefficient. A term with no variable is a constant term. For example, in the expression 5x^{2} - 3x + 7, the terms are...
- **Like Terms** --- Like terms are terms that have exactly the same variable parts (same variables raised to the same powers). Only like terms can be combined.

## Key Properties

- **Combining Like Terms** --- To combine like terms, add or subtract their coefficients and keep the variable part unchanged: \[ ax^{n} + bx^{n} = (a + b)x^{n}. \]
