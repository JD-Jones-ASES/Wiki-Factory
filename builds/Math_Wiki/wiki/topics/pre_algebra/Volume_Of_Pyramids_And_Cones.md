# Volume of Pyramids and Cones

## Definitions

- **Pyramid** --- A pyramid is a solid with one polygonal base and triangular lateral faces that meet at a common vertex. A pyramid is named by the shape of its base (for example, a square pyramid).
- **Cone** --- A cone is a solid with one circular base and a curved lateral surface that tapers to a point (the apex).

## Key Properties

- **Volume of a Pyramid** --- For a pyramid with base area~B and height~h: \[ V = 1{3}Bh \]
- **Volume of a Cone** --- For a cone with radius~r and height~h: \[ V = 1{3} r^2 h \]
