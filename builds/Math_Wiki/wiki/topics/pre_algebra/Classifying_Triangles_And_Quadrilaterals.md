# Classifying Triangles and Quadrilaterals

## Definitions

- **Triangles by Sides** --- itemize[nosep] Equilateral: all three sides are equal. Isosceles: at least two sides are equal. Scalene: no sides are equal. itemize
- **Triangles by Angles** --- itemize[nosep] Acute triangle: all three angles are less than 90. Right triangle: one angle is exactly 90. Obtuse triangle: one angle is greater than 90. itemize

## Key Properties

- **Triangle Angle Sum** --- The sum of the interior angles of any triangle is 180. \[ m A + m B + m C = 180. \]
- **Quadrilateral Angle Sum** --- The sum of the four interior angles of any quadrilateral is 360.
