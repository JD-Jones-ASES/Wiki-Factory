## Definitions

- **Prism** --- A prism is a solid with two parallel, congruent polygonal bases and rectangular lateral faces. A prism is named by the shape of its bases.
- **Net** --- A net is a two-dimensional pattern that can be folded to form a three-dimensional solid. Nets are helpful for visualizing surface area.

## Key Properties

- **Surface Area of a Rectangular Prism** --- For a rectangular prism with length~l, width~w, and height~h: \[ SA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh \]
- **Surface Area of a Cylinder** --- For a cylinder with radius~r and height~h: \[ SA = 2 r^2 + 2 rh \]
