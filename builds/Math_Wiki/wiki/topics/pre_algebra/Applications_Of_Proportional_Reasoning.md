## Definitions

- **Conversion Factor** --- A conversion factor is a ratio of two equivalent measurements, such as 12 in.{1 ft} or 1 km{1{,}000 m}. Because the two measurements are equal, a conversion factor equals~1.
- **Similar Figures** --- Two figures are similar if they have the same shape but not necessarily the same size. Corresponding angles are equal, and corresponding sides are proportional.

## Key Properties

- **Solving with a Conversion Factor** --- To convert between units, set up a proportion using the conversion factor: \[ known unit/desired unit = given quantity/x \] Then cross multiply and solve.
- **Percent as a Proportion** --- A percent problem can be written as the proportion \[ part/whole = percent/100 \] where the percent is the number before the \% sign.
