# Divisibility, Factors, and Prime Factorization

## Definitions

- **Divisibility** --- A whole number~a is divisible by a nonzero whole number~b if a b has a remainder of~0. We also say ``b~divides~a.''
- **Factor** --- A factor of a whole number is a number that divides it evenly (with remainder~0). For example, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12 are the factors of~12.

- **Prime Factorization** --- The prime factorization of a whole number writes it as a product of prime numbers. A factor tree is a diagram used to find the prime factorization.
