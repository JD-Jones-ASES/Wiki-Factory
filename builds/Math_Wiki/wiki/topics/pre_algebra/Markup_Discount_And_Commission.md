# Markup, Discount, and Commission

## Definitions

- **Markup** --- The markup is the amount added to the cost of an item to obtain the selling price: \[ selling price = cost + markup. \] The markup rate is the percent of the cost: markup = rate cost.

- **Commission** --- A commission is an amount earned based on total sales: \[ commission = commission rate total sales. \]
