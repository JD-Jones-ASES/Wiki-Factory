## Definitions

- **Arithmetic Sequence** --- An arithmetic sequence is a sequence in which the difference between consecutive terms is constant. This constant is called the common difference d. \[ d = a_{n+1} - a_n \]

## Key Properties

- **Explicit Formula for an Arithmetic Sequence** --- The nth term of an arithmetic sequence with first term a_1 and common difference d is \[ a_n = a_1 + (n - 1)d. \]
