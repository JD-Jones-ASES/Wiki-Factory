# Geometry

**The high school geometry course. Shapes, measurement, proof reasoning, and coordinate geometry.**

Geometry is where math becomes visual and where reasoning becomes the point. You will measure and classify the figures you already know, learn exactly why the Pythagorean theorem works, prove triangles congruent with a handful of criteria, watch shapes translate and rotate under coordinate rules, and compute the volume and surface area of every solid you can think of.

> **Heads up:** this hub currently lists the geometry-adjacent topics that share the pre-algebra foundations (perimeter, area, Pythagorean theorem, triangle basics, coordinate plane). The full HS Geometry cluster --- triangle congruence, parallel lines and transversals, circle theorems, rigid transformations, 3D volume and surface area --- is the next major build phase.

---

## What You'll Learn

- **Triangle geometry** --- classifying, triangle angle sum, similar triangles, the Pythagorean theorem and its applications, (coming soon) triangle congruence criteria.
- **Polygons** --- perimeter, area, classifying quadrilaterals, composite figures, (coming soon) polygon angle sums and quadrilateral properties.
- **Circles** --- circumference, area, (coming soon) inscribed angles, chord/secant/tangent relationships, equations of circles.
- **Measurement in 3D** --- surface area and volume of prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones, and spheres.
- **Coordinate geometry** --- plotting points, distance and midpoint, (coming soon) coordinate proofs and equations of circles.
- **Transformations** --- (coming soon) rigid transformations, dilations, similarity, and symmetry.
