## How to Use This Wiki

1. **Pick your course** from the list above and open its hub.
2. **Read the lesson** --- intuition first, then the rule, then two or three worked examples.
3. **Scroll to "Problems Involving This Topic"** and click **Add to Vault** on a few problems.
4. **Open your [[Vault]]** to review hints, answers, or print a worksheet.
5. **Repeat.** Practice compounds.

The Vault is yours and yours alone. No accounts, no tracking, no uploads --- it lives in your browser's local storage. Clear your browser data and the Vault clears too.
