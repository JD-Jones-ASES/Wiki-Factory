## How to Use This Wiki

1. **Pick a topic** from a Learning Path above (or browse a branch hub).
2. **Read the lesson** --- intuition first, then the rule, then two or three worked examples.
3. **Scroll to "Problems Involving This Topic"** and click **Add to Vault** on a few problems.
4. **Open your [[Vault]]** whenever you're ready to practice --- it remembers what you saved.
5. **Download a worksheet** from the Vault (browser print → PDF works today; a polished PDF button is on the roadmap).

The Vault lives entirely in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded. Clear your browser data and the Vault clears too.
