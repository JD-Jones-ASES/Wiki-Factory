# Pre-Calculus & Trigonometry

**The course that gets you ready for calculus. Everything-you-need-before-derivatives, with trigonometry baked in.**

Pre-Calculus is where you meet all the functions calculus assumes you are fluent in. You will work the full unit circle, prove trig identities, solve trig equations, pick up the law of sines and law of cosines, handle sequences and summation notation, learn the binomial theorem and mathematical induction, work polar coordinates, do arithmetic with complex numbers (including De Moivre's theorem), and solve systems with matrices.

---

## What You'll Learn

- **Trigonometry** --- radian measure, the unit circle, the six trig functions, inverse trig functions, identities, trig equations, graphs of sinusoids, law of sines, law of cosines.
- **Vectors** --- components, magnitude, direction, dot product.
- **Sequences and series** --- arithmetic and geometric sequences, sigma (summation) notation, mathematical induction.
- **Combinatorics and the binomial theorem.**
- **Polar coordinates and complex numbers** --- polar form, De Moivre's theorem, complex zeros of polynomials.
- **Matrices** --- arithmetic, augmented matrices, determinants, matrix methods for solving systems.
