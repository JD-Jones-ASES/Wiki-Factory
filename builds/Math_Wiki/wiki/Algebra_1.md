# Algebra 1

**The first full year of high school algebra. Linear functions, quadratics, and everything in between.**

Algebra 1 is where symbols do real work. You learn to solve equations and inequalities rigorously, graph lines and write their equations, handle systems of two unknowns, manipulate exponents and polynomials, factor, and solve your first quadratics. By the end of the course you can look at an equation and a graph and say, "these two describe the same thing."

---

## What You'll Learn

- **Linear equations** --- one-step, multi-step, with variables on both sides, and absolute-value equations.
- **Linear inequalities** --- one- and two-step, compound, absolute-value, and systems of inequalities.
- **Lines and linear functions** --- slope, intercepts, slope-intercept form, point-slope form, parallel and perpendicular lines, scatter plots and trend lines.
- **Systems** --- substitution, elimination, modeling with systems.
- **Exponents and polynomials** --- exponent rules, polynomial arithmetic, special products, factoring.
- **Quadratics** --- solving by factoring, by square roots, by the quadratic formula.
- **First functions** --- relations vs. functions, function notation, arithmetic of functions.
