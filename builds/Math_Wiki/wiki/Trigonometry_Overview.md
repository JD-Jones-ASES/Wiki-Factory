# Trigonometry Overview

> [[_overview|Home]] > Trigonometry

Trigonometry started as the study of triangles — specifically, the ratios of their sides. But it grew into something much bigger: a language for describing anything that oscillates (waves, seasons, orbits, pendulums, sound). Master the unit circle and you'll see these patterns everywhere.

---

## What You'll Learn

- **Right-triangle trig:** sine, cosine, tangent ratios; angles of elevation and depression
- **The unit circle:** radian measure, circular function definitions
- **Trig identities:** Pythagorean, sum/difference, double-angle, half-angle
- **Trig equations:** solving equations that involve sine, cosine, tangent
- **Law of Sines and Law of Cosines:** solving oblique triangles
- **Graphs of trig functions:** amplitude, period, phase shift
