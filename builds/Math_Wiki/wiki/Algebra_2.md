# Algebra 2

**The second year of high school algebra. Functions become the main character.**

Algebra 2 takes everything from Algebra 1 and lifts it one level up. Quadratics become a family of parabolas you can transform and analyze. Polynomials get longer and more interesting. Rational, radical, exponential, and logarithmic functions all join the roster. By the end of the course you think in terms of **function families**, and you can move any graph around the coordinate plane by reading one equation.

---

## What You'll Learn

- **Quadratics in depth** --- completing the square, graphing parabolas with transformations, applications and optimization.
- **Function fundamentals** --- function notation, arithmetic and composition, inverse functions.
- **Function families and transformations** --- absolute value, power, polynomial, rational, exponential, logarithmic, and piecewise functions, all transformed consistently.
- **Polynomials beyond quadratics** --- factoring, graphing, and solving higher-degree polynomials.
- **Rational expressions and equations** --- simplifying, combining, solving, and graphing rational functions.
- **Radicals** --- rational exponents, simplifying radical expressions, solving radical equations, graphing radical functions.
- **Exponentials and logarithms** --- growth and decay, exponential equations, logarithmic properties, applications including compound interest.
- **Conic sections and complex numbers** --- parabolas, ellipses, hyperbolas, and the complex number system.
