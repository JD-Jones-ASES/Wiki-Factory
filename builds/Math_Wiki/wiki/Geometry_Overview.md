# Geometry Overview

> [[_overview|Home]] > Geometry

Geometry is the study of shapes, space, and measurement. It's the branch of math where you can see the answers — angles that fit together, triangles that scale, circles with hidden symmetry. Geometry also teaches the art of **proof**: showing why something must be true.

---

## What You'll Learn

- **Foundations:** points, lines, planes, angles
- **Triangles:** classification, congruence, similarity, special right triangles
- **Polygons:** quadrilaterals, regular polygons, angle sums
- **Circles:** equations, chords, arcs, inscribed angles
- **Coordinate geometry:** distance, midpoint, slope, equations of lines and circles
- **Solid geometry:** volume, surface area, cross-sections
- **Transformations:** translations, rotations, reflections, dilations
